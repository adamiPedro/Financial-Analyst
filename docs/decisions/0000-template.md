# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-name.md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced this decision. Constraints, pressures, what was true at the time.
Write it so it still makes sense to someone who wasn't here — including you in
four months.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and in the active voice. "We store the filing
accession number in the uniqueness key", not "it was decided that...".

## Consequences

**Good**
- what this buys

**Bad**
- what this costs, honestly
- an ADR with no costs listed is a sales pitch, not a decision record

## Alternatives considered

**Option name** — why it was rejected. One or two sentences each.

This section is the one interviewers read. A decision with no rejected
alternatives reads as a default that was never examined.
